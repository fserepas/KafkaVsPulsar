Pulsar/MQTT handler NARs, JDBC drivers, JMX exporter, Maven dependencies and container images also carry their own embedded/upstream notices. Retain those when distributing corresponding binaries. A source repository with an independent project license does not replace the connector licenses. Review the evaluation connector's entitlement/redistribution terms before publishing a runtime-asset release.
